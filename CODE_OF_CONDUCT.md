# Code of Conduct

Comic Viewer welcomes anyone who wants to report a bug, suggest an idea or contribute code. Keep
issues, pull requests and comments friendly and on topic, so everyone feels free to take part.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent, including when you disagree.
- Give feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Stay on the topic of the issue or pull request.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats or personal attacks.
- Jokes or language that demean anyone for who they are.
- Sexual content, or violent content aimed at people.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, advertising, and links to pirated comics.

## Reporting

Use **Report content** in the menu (⋯) of the comment, issue or pull request; GitHub reviews
reports under its [Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines).
To reach the maintainer privately, open a
[private report](https://github.com/Niklaus1911/ComicViewer/security/advisories/new) and say that
it is about conduct. Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens

Depending on how serious the behavior is, the maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, lock
conversations, close issues and pull requests, and block accounts from the repository. This code
applies to everything in this repository, and GitHub's Community Guidelines apply as well.
