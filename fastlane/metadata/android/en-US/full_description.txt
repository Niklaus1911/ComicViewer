Comic Viewer is a fast, private reader for the comics already on your device: CBZ and ZIP archives, PDF comics and folders of images. Pages are read straight from your files, nothing is extracted or uploaded, and no account is needed.

YOUR LIBRARY
• Add comics one by one, scan a folder you choose, or let the app find CBZ and ZIP files on your device
• Pick up where you stopped from Continue reading, with progress on every cover
• Search by title, series, writer or genre, read from the ComicInfo.xml inside your archives
• Grid, compact and list layouts, sorted by date added, title or last read
• A details page for every comic with page previews, page count, file size and metadata
• Reading history grouped by day, with finished comics marked

READING
• Swipe or tap the edges to turn pages, or scroll vertically through webtoons and long strips
• Pinch to zoom, with pages reloaded in sharper detail as you zoom in
• A page thumbnail grid to jump anywhere in the comic
• Full-screen reading with the screen kept on and an optional page number
• Layouts for phones and tablets, in portrait and landscape

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works offline with the files on your device or picked from your storage apps
• No account, no ads, no uploads
• Back up and restore your library, progress and settings

COMIC VIEWER PRO
A one-time purchase unlocks:
• Two-page spreads and right-to-left manga reading
• Fit width, fit height and original size
• Reader backgrounds, brightness and margins
• Favorites, tags and progress filters
• Extra color themes
Vertical scrolling stays free.

The app is available in English and Italian, and TalkBack announces each page by its position, such as "Page 10 of 24".
